Our Rendering: The Islands Beneath the Rainbow

October 29, 2026 · KHPA Public Information · 3 min read

This rendering shows the Hawaiian archipelago as one continuous chain, with a rainbow (ʻānuenue) arcing above it. It is the artwork planned for KHPA license plates.

Reading the rendering

The chain is laid out as it lies on the map, with the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands trailing off toward the upper left and Hawaiʻi Island anchoring the lower right. Each main island has its own color:

The main islands in the rendering
IslandWhere to find it
HawaiʻiRed, at the lower right. The largest island.
MauiPink, to the northwest of Hawaiʻi Island.
KahoʻolawePale lavender, a small island just south of Maui.
LānaʻiOrange, between Maui and Molokaʻi.
MolokaʻiGreen, long and narrow, north of Lānaʻi.
OʻahuGold, to the northwest of Molokaʻi.
KauaʻiPurple, the northwesternmost of the main islands.
NiʻihauA small island southwest of Kauaʻi.

The whole chain

The rendering includes the Northwestern Hawaiian Islands alongside the eight main islands, so the full length of the archipelago, more than 1,500 miles, is in view. The small islands and atolls at the upper left are ringed by turquoise reef and include the places described in Papahānaumokuākea: The Northwestern Chain.

The rainbow

A rainbow begins faintly at the far northwestern end of the chain and arcs across the archipelago, ending near Hawaiʻi Island. A fainter second band runs beneath the main arc.

Plates, registration, and questions

For questions about registration and licensing, see Registration & Licensing. Requirements vary by category and are confirmed by office staff, so contact your island office before visiting, or call the public line at (808) 333-3459.

To learn more about the land shown here, read The Hawaiian Archipelago.
