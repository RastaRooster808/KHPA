How Verification Works

Standard

Each KHPA ID card QR code carries the ID number, name and expiration date, sealed with a KHPA digital signature. This page checks that signature on your own device, so it works without any online records, and any change to the card details makes the check fail. Emergency contacts are added by the card holder and are not checked by KHPA.
