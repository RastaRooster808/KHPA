Emporium

The KHPA Emporium provides informational listings for approved community offerings and affiliated providers. This page serves as a public reference portal and does not function as a direct transaction platform.

Official Notice: KHPA does not conduct direct sales, collect payments, or process transactions through this page. Listings are presented for informational purposes and are subject to Kingdom protocol and administrative review.

Affiliation & Purpose

Public
  • Affiliation
    The KHPA Emporium is a product of Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina (KHPA).
  • Purpose
    The Emporium provides informational listings for community offerings, independent providers, and approved affiliated expressions.
  • Eligibility Requirement
    Applicants must hold a valid KHPA Business Identification and KHPA Business Registration prior to submission.
  • Submission & Processing
    Emporium applications shall be submitted in accordance with Kingdom Protocol and shall be received, reviewed, and processed through KHPA.

Hale Kūʻai

Hāʻawi ka Hale Kūʻai KHPA i nā papa ʻike no nā mea hāʻawi kaiāulu i ʻae ʻia a me nā mea pili kūhelu. He ʻaoʻao ʻike lehulehu kēia, ʻaʻole ia he kahua kūʻai pololei.

Hoʻolaha Kūhelu: ʻAʻole mālama ka KHPA i ke kūʻai pololei, ka ʻohi uku, a me ka hoʻoponopono kālepa ma kēia ʻaoʻao. Hōʻike ʻia nā papa inoa no ka ʻike wale nō, a pili lākou i ke Kingdom Protocol a me ka nānā hoʻokele.

Pili Kūlana & Kumu

Lehulehu
  • Pili Kūlana
    He huahana ka Hale Kūʻai KHPA na Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina (KHPA).
  • Kumu
    Hāʻawi ka Hale Kūʻai i nā papa ʻike no nā mea hāʻawi kaiāulu, nā mea lawelawe kūʻokoʻa, a me nā hōʻike pili i ʻae ʻia.
  • Koi Komo
    Pono i ka mea noi ke loaʻa he KHPA Business Identification kūpono a me KHPA Business Registration kūpono ma mua o ka waiho ʻana.
  • Waiho & Hoʻoponopono
    E waiho ʻia nā noi no ka Hale Kūʻai ma lalo o ke Kingdom Protocol, a e nānā ʻia a e hoʻoponopono ʻia ma o ka KHPA.
