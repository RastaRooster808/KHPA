Registration & Licensing

Pre-Check Notice: The forms listed below are provided as pre-information / pre-check forms only. Online submission through this website is not available.

Kākau & Laikini

Hoʻolaha Mua-Nānā: Hāʻawi ʻia nā pepa ma lalo nei no ka mua-ʻike / mua-nānā wale nō. ʻAʻole hiki ke waiho noi ma o kēia pūnaewele.

Waiho & Hoʻoponopono: E waiho a e hoʻoponopono ʻia nā noi ma o ka KHPA. Pono ka mea noi e kelepona i ke keʻena a i ʻole kipa kino i ka wā hana no nā kuhikuhi a me ka hoʻoponopono ʻana.
