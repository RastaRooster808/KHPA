Registration & Licensing

Public service categories, pre-check forms, and submission instructions for identification, motor vehicle, vehicle and vessel registration, business registration, vital records, and wallet credential reference.

Pre-Check Notice: The forms listed below are provided as pre-information / pre-check forms only. Online submission through this website is not available. Applications shall be submitted and processed through KHPA. Applicants must call the office or visit in person during operating hours for instructions and processing.
Submit at Island Office: Every registration and licensing matter is finalized in person or by phone at a KHPA island office. The pre-check forms and PDFs on this page help you prepare — they are not a final submission on their own. See Island Offices for routing and contact information.

How to Apply

Step by Step

  1. Review Your Category

    Find the section below that matches what you need: identification, motor vehicle, vehicle/vessel, business, or vital records.

  2. Complete the Pre-Check Form

    Fill out the online pre-check form or download the PDF application listed for that category.

  3. Gather Your Documents

    Collect the items in the Required Documents Checklist below before you call or visit.

  4. Submit at Island Office

    Call or visit your island office to finalize the application. Pre-check forms alone do not complete your request.

Required Documents Checklist

Prepare Ahead

Requirements can vary by service category and are confirmed by office staff. Contact your island office before your visit to confirm the exact documents needed for your request.

  • Valid photo identification
  • Completed pre-check form or printed PDF application for your service category
  • Any existing KHPA certificate, registration, or reference number related to your request
  • Supporting documents specific to your category (see the reference PDFs in each section below)
  • Current contact information for follow-up (phone number and/or email address)
