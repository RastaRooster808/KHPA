Emporium

Affiliation: The KHPA Emporium is a product of Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina (KHPA).

Purpose: The Emporium provides informational listings for community offerings. KHPA does not conduct sales, accept payment, or process transactions through this page.

Eligibility Requirement: Applicants must hold a valid KHPA Business Identification and KHPA Business Registration prior to submission.

Submission & Processing: Emporium applications shall be submitted in accordance with Kingdom Protocol and shall be received, reviewed, and processed through KHPA.

External Link: This page includes a link to RastaRooster.com.

Hale Kūʻai

Pili Kūlana: He huahana ka Hale Kūʻai KHPA na Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina (KHPA).

Kumu: Hāʻawi kēia ʻaoʻao i ka ʻike no nā papa inoa pili kaiāulu. ʻAʻole mālama ka KHPA i ke kūʻai aku, ka ʻae uku, a i ʻole ka hoʻoponopono kālepa ma kēia ʻaoʻao.

Koi Komo: Pono i ka mea noi ke loaʻa he KHPA Business Identification a me KHPA Business Registration kūpono ma mua o ka waiho ʻana.

Waiho & Hoʻoponopono: E waiho ʻia nā noi no ka Hale Kūʻai ma lalo o ka Kingdom Protocol, a e nānā ʻia a e hoʻoponopono ʻia ma o ka KHPA.

Loulou Kūwaho: Aia ma kēia ʻaoʻao he loulou i RastaRooster.com.
