Ceremonial Declaration

Decree of Administrative Order and Public Access

Let it be publicly known that the island offices listed herein are presented for ceremonial recognition, administrative order, and accessible communication within Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina.

These offices stand as points of routing, guidance, and reception for matters concerning documents, protocol, registration, island correspondence, and public assistance.

All persons approaching these channels are asked to proceed with dignity, respect, and observance of proper protocol, so that communication may remain orderly, transparent, and beneficial to the people and the ʻāina.
