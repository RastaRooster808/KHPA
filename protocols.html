Protocol

KHPA Protocol is the orderly process by which submissions, documents, requests, and office communications are prepared, routed, reviewed, and answered through the proper channels with dignity, clarity, and respect.

Official Notice: Public guidance does not constitute automatic approval, recognition, or completion of process. All matters remain subject to review through proper office channels.

Protocol Summary

Standard

This page provides a quick and practical list for public submissions, office preparation, routing, and orderly communication through Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina.

Quick List

Public Use
  • 1. Review the correct page first
    Use the proper service page before sending documents or requests
    Registration
  • 2. Use complete contact information
    Include full name, email address, phone number, and island routing area when applicable
  • 3. State the purpose clearly
    Identify whether the matter concerns registration, government documents, office routing, protocol, or general assistance
  • 4. Select the correct island office
    Direct the matter to the proper island office or general contact channel
    Island Offices
  • 5. Submit only relevant materials
    Provide only the forms, records, and supporting information necessary for review
  • 6. Keep copies of all submissions
    Retain copies of forms, messages, and supporting records for your own files
  • 7. Await proper review
    Do not send repeated duplicate submissions before reasonable review time has passed
  • 8. Follow official communication channels
    Use office email, direct assistance, and listed contact points for follow-up
    Contact
  • 9. Observe dignity and order
    All communications should proceed with respect, truthfulness, clarity, and aloha

Submission Protocol

Checklist
  • Correct Service
    Confirm that the request belongs under registration, documents, protocol, or general routing
  • Correct Information
    Make sure names, contact details, and subject lines are accurate and complete
  • Correct Materials
    Attach only the records necessary for review and keep personal copies
  • Correct Routing
    Choose the appropriate island office or general administrative contact
