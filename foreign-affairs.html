Office of Foreign Affairs

The Office of Foreign Affairs provides public notice, external communication, and administrative reference for posted KHPA signage, restricted entry, and appointment-based access.

Official Notice: Posted KHPA signage serves as public notice. Entry into restricted areas is by appointment only and subject to KHPA protocol.

Official Signage Acknowledgement

Posted Notice

Ko Hawaiʻi Pae ʻĀina (KHPA) acknowledges the posted restricted-area notice shown below as official signage for public awareness, controlled access, and administrative routing.

Members of the public are expected to respect posted notices before entering, approaching, or requesting access to designated areas. Entry is by appointment only and must follow proper KHPA office procedure.

Restricted Entry: Areas bearing this signage are subject to appointment-based access, administrative control, and protocol observance. Unauthorized entry is not permitted.

Entry Policy

Protocol
  • Appointment Required
    Entry into posted areas is by appointment only.
  • Administrative Review
    Access requests may require review, routing, and confirmation through KHPA offices.
  • Respect for ʻĀina
    All persons are expected to observe cultural respect, stewardship, and proper protocol.
  • Posted Notice
    Posted signage provides public notice of restricted access and controlled entry.
