Official Public Portal

This portal provides public access to KHPA registration and licensing services, government documents, island office routing, judiciary appointments, and administrative assistance — organized for clear, orderly, and respectful public access across all islands.

Administrative Notice: KHPA office locations are in the process of relocating. Updated location details will be posted once finalized. KHPA is also pursuing formal registration of an official .gov domain. Current contact numbers remain in service — see the Island Offices page for the full record.
