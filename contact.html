The Power of the People

Our strength is our people. Across every island, families and communities carry the kuleana to care for one another and for the land. This page connects you to the island offices that serve you. Stand together. Act with dignity. Build our future.

Unity of the Pae ʻAina: One identity, many islands — with a shared purpose: protection, service, and lawful self-determination. Mālama is most important: to care for the land, the people, and one another with steadiness and respect.
